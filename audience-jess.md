## Platforms

Fanvue, FeetFinder, and subscription platforms for monetization. TikTok as discovery and top of funnel. LiveGood affiliate offer runs under Jess.

## Audience A, the paying subscriber or fan

The person who pays Jess. Understanding why they pay is the whole game.

### What they actually want

Connection, not just content. "The subscriber feels like he has an actual personal connection with the model, has her attention and perhaps under the delusion that their connection is something special." (BlackHatWorld)

A relationship that lasts. "There's a difference between watching porn and messaging with a model or receiving custom content from her. It builds a relationship between the buyer and the model, which sometimes can keep on going for years." (BlackHatWorld)

Direct access with no middle layer. They pay "for the attention, to interact, to support the content creator," for content "that's raw, real, and often completely different from what you'll find anywhere else. No algorithms, no noise, just direct access to the people you choose to follow." (Spocket)

Personalization. "you can request unique content, such as personalised advice." (Getsby)

### What this means for Jess

The product is attention and relationship, not just images. Custom requests, direct messages, and the feeling of being chosen are what convert and retain. Subscribers who feel a personal connection stay for years, so retention beats one time sales. Lead with access and personality, the Gen X Unleashed confidence is the draw.

## Audience B, the aspiring creator

Women who want to become a creator like Jess and would buy a how to, a starter system, or coaching from her.

### Their hopes, grounded in real numbers

The income is real but modest at first. On FeetFinder "beginners typically earn between $100 to $500 per month, and with marketing and consistency, some sellers scale to $1,000 to $5,000 monthly." Feet pics sell "for anywhere between $5 to $30." (FeetFinder seller reviews)

It works only when treated seriously. "FeetFinder can be profitable but only if treated like a business, not as instant money, requiring marketing, branding, and consistency." (startupbooted)

### Their fears, also real

The quiet failure they are scared of. "For every successful creator interview, there are thousands who launched pages, posted for three months, made $47, and quietly deleted everything." (Vicesnob)

The hard truth about the odds. "the median creator makes about $180 per month." The market is "a tiny group earning enormous money, a middle class grinding it out, and a massive base earning almost nothing." (SuperCreator)

Fees and payout delays. FeetFinder takes "20% commission" plus a subscription, and payout delays "are the most documented issue." Fanvue counters with "an 85/15 fee split" and a "7-day payout" against OnlyFans "21-day, 20% fee." (FeetFinder reviews, SuperCreator)

### What this means for Jess

Sell the truth, not the dream. This audience has heard the hype and is scared of being the one who made $47 and quit. Jess wins by being the honest sharper voice who says treat it like a business, here is the system, here is what month one really looks like. Position Fanvue's better split and faster payout as the smarter starting point. The promise is a real first $100 to $500 with a repeatable system, not overnight money.
