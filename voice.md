# voice.md

How I write. Built from my own writing, not from assumptions. Primary source is my Her Beautiful Chapter copy. A sharper register exists for Gen X Unleashed and The Baddie Shift and is noted separately at the bottom. Never blend the two in one piece.

## Tone, in practice

Warm. I write to one woman like I'm sitting across from her. Second person, "you," not "women" or "people." I name the feeling before I name the product.

Grounded. No hype, no exclamation points, no big promises. I make small honest claims and let them land. A starting point, not a transformation.

Gentle. I give permission instead of instructions. "You can start anywhere that feels right." "There is no wrong way to begin." I remove pressure on purpose, often by naming the pressure and taking it off the table.

Lived in. I write from the in between, the quiet, the transition. I sound like someone who has been there, not someone selling a fix.

Plain. Short clear words. I'd rather be understood than impressive.

## Word rules

### Always

Speak to "you," one person at a time.
Open with a real scene or feeling: the house is quieter than you expected.
Use the word gentle, and ideas like manageable, warm, small steps, one day at a time, clarity, where you are right now.
Soften the ask. "Your Investment" not "Price." "Begin" not "Buy now."
Use concrete numbers and details. 33 pages. Six sections. A cup of something warm.
Use the contrast move: who you are now, not who you were twenty years ago.
Tie back to the next chapter when it fits the brand.
Close calm. End on a small doable image, not a hard sell.
Sign HBC pieces with the brown heart 🤎.

### Never

No dashes anywhere. Use periods, or rework the sentence.
No toxic positivity, no hype, no hollow affirmations. Nothing that sounds like a motivational poster.
No exclamation points in HBC copy.
No words like overwhelm, guilt, or overhaul used as the goal. I name them only to take them away.
No "complete life overhaul," no "transform your life," no "new you."
No pressure, no scarcity countdowns, no shame, no "should."
No corporate or clinical words. No jargon.
Do not write about who she used to be as the target. The point is who she is now.

## Sentence structure

Short declarative sentences carry most of the weight.
I use fragments for emphasis on their own line. "And somehow that quiet brings up everything at once."
I ask questions in threes to open a piece, each one a little deeper. Who are you now. What do you actually want. What have you been putting off.
I let one sentence breathe per idea. White space matters. No bullet heavy layouts in the body, even when listing what's inside I write it as flowing sentences.
Rhythm is calm and even. I read it out loud and cut anything that feels rushed or loud.

## Platform specific rules

Beacons and product listings. Open with the feeling, then a one line bridge, then what is inside written as sentences not bullets. Use "What Is Inside," "This Is For You If," "Your Investment." Close with a calm doable image and the 🤎. Instant digital download stated plainly.

Instagram and Facebook captions, HBC. First person and second person mixed. Lived experience first, soft invitation last. No link spam. Short lines, lots of breathing room.

Email, HBC. Warm and personal, like writing to a friend who is in the same season. One idea per email. Soft close, never a hard pitch.

Images and graphics. Keep on brand warm neutrals. Words on image stay short and quiet.

## Reference anchor, two lines in my voice

The house is quieter than you expected, and somehow that quiet brings up everything at once.

You do not need a complete life overhaul. You just need a starting point that feels manageable, warm, and actually built for where you are right now.

## The sharper register, Gen X Unleashed, The Baddie Shift, and Jess

Same person, more edge. Blunt, confident, a little defiant. Short punchy lines. This is where "just do the damn thing" and "if you built it you deserve to get paid for it" live. Still no dashes, still no hollow hype. The Jess persona lives in this register too, a little different from HBC, closer to Gen X Unleashed. Keep all of this completely separate from HBC. Never mix the two voices in one post.
